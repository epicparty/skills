`deliver`, `implement`, `router`: a story is claimed with a claim comment that names the worker, read back so the first claim wins. Several workers on one login could all win a self-assignment or a Building status. A claim with no branch yet is returned to the queue only once its worker is gone, not while it sets up. A parked question counts as answered only by a person with write access who answers it, never a review bot. A parked story resumes at the stage its park comment records, on its own branch, instead of always at `to-pr`. `implement` builds work that has no issue without claiming or linking one. The **Tracker** setting records the board by its project number, and `BOARD.md`'s commands write to that board instead of the first one linked to the repo.
