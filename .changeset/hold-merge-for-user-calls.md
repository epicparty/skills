`router`, `babysit`, `to-pr`, `implement`: a call left to the user holds the merge. **Open question** now covers a call you left *to* the user, such as whether the work crosses a line the request drew, as well as one you made for them: every call put to the user about the work is one. **Found in passing** says what can wait until after the merge: a finding that would be its own story either way. The change's own follow-through, such as a list or a doc it leaves stale, lands as a commit, or is an open question where a line drawn around the work leaves it out. The merge gate reads **Found in passing** and the replies on the PR for any such call, moves it to an open question, and holds.
