`code-review`, `explore`, `to-story`: a review of one commit reads that commit (`git show`), a range reads its two endpoints, and working changes include untracked files. `explore` reads the issue the user named, with its comments, and `to-story` refines it instead of filing another. A split reads **Tracker** before publishing and only prints the stories under **None**, and its frontier also drops a child whose `## Blocked by` list names an open issue.
